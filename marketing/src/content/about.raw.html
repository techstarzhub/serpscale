Who We Are

Making Professional SEO Affordable for Every Team

SerpScale started with a simple frustration: agencies were paying for SEMrush, Ahrefs, a separate rank tracker and a reporting tool just to run their campaigns. We set out to build one all-in-one SEO platform that replaces that entire stack — a rank tracker, site audit, backlink checker and keyword research together in a single dashboard, at a price small teams can actually justify.
