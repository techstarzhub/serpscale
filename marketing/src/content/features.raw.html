CORE SEO TOOLS

Research, Track & Analyze — All in One Place

Keyword Rank Tracker

Track daily keyword positions on Google across desktop and mobile, by country and city. See visibility trends, rank deltas and full history, synced live with Google Search Console.

Keyword Research

Turn one seed keyword into thousands of ideas with real monthly search volume, keyword difficulty and search intent. Instantly see who ranks for any term.

Competitor Analysis

See the domains competing for your keywords, their common keywords, estimated traffic and the exact keyword gaps you can target to overtake them.

Backlink Checker

Monitor backlinks and referring domains, catch toxic links early, and track spam score and domain rank — then benchmark your authority against competitors.

Site Audit

Crawl every page, score overall site health, and get a prioritized, AI-assisted list of technical SEO fixes and Core Web Vitals issues to work through.

Domain Intelligence

Domain authority score, detected technology stack, hosting and contact signals — a full snapshot of any domain at a glance.
